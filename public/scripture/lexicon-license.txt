public/scripture/lexicon/words/*.json
  Word-by-word text for each book.
  New Testament: Translators Amalgamated Greek NT (TAGNT).
  Only words present in the Textus Receptus (Scrivener 1894) are included.
  STEP Bible marks those words with K outside parentheses. Where the file
  records a distinct Textus Receptus spelling, that spelling is the form shown.
  Old Testament: Translators Amalgamated Hebrew OT (TAHOT), the Leningrad
  reading (type L), including Aramaic in Daniel and Ezra when that reading
  is marked Aramaic.
  Morpheme slashes, cantillation, and verse punctuation are not shown.
  A hyphen in the Hebrew transliteration marks a prefix. A capital letter
  is the stressed syllable in the STEP Bible transliteration.
  Strong's numbers are written without leading zeros. A final letter, such
  as H1254A, is STEP Bible's disambiguation of that number.

public/scripture/lexicon/greek.json
  Translators Brief lexicon of Extended Strongs for Greek (TBESG).
  Brief definitions from Abbott-Smith where STEP Bible used that lexicon,
  and from other brief definitions where Abbott-Smith had none.
  Abbott-Smith's Manual Greek Lexicon of the New Testament (1922) is in the
  public domain in the United States. The STEP Bible compilation is CC BY 4.0.

public/scripture/lexicon/hebrew.json
  Translators Brief lexicon of Extended Strongs for Hebrew (TBESH).
  Abridged Brown-Driver-Briggs definitions. BDB (1906) is in the public
  domain. The STEP Bible abridgement is CC BY 4.0.

Changes recorded for CC BY 4.0:
  The files were reformatted as JSON and split by book so a phone can load
  one book at a time. Morphology codes, edition lists, and readings that are
  not the Textus Receptus or the Leningrad text were left out. Definitions
  longer than about 900 Greek characters or 1,200 Hebrew characters were cut
  at a sentence or a line. Markup for bold, line breaks, and Abbott-Smith
  sense numbers was turned into plain text. Related words are Strong's
  numbers cited in the definition, plus a Greek lemma the definition already
  names when that lemma is in this lexicon. The wording of the definitions
  was not rewritten.

STEP Bible asks projects to point back to github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data
as the place where corrections are collected. These files are the extract
this app needs offline. They are not a mirror of the full datasets.
