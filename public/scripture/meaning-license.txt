CCEL labels that text "Rights: Public Domain".

The same public-domain text is distributed by the CrossWire Bible Society
as the SWORD module MHCC (DistributionLicense=Public Domain):
https://crosswire.org/sword/modules/ModInfo.jsp?modName=MHCC

public/scripture/meaning/*.json is that commentary, split by book.
Each note keeps the verse or verse range it was written for.
Book introductions that are not a chapter note, chapter outlines, and the
index were left out. CCEL markers written as #(n) were restored to the
verse numbers. A leading "--" used as a print dash was removed.
The wording of the notes was not rewritten.
See scripts/build-meaning.mjs.
