Study notes beyond Matthew Henry's Concise Commentary

In context
  Matthew Henry's Concise Commentary on the Whole Bible. Public domain.
  See public/scripture/meaning-license.txt.
  The whole note is shown under "In context". It was not split into an
  original-audience section or a today section. The commentary does not
  label those parts, and this app does not invent the division.

Then (original audience)
  unfoldingWord Translation Notes, version 85.

  Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
  https://git.door43.org/unfoldingWord/en_tn/releases/tag/v85

  public/scripture/study/then/*.json is an extract, not the whole resource.
  From each book's introduction, the kept sections are the ones the source
  already separates as the author, the occasion, and "Important Religious
  and Cultural Concepts" (Part 2, or a heading of that kind).
  Left out: outlines, "how should the title be translated", "special
  formatting", and Part 3 "Important Translation Issues", including any
  subsection whose heading is about translation or textual issues.
  The kept sentences were not rewritten. Markdown links and emphasis were
  removed so the note can be shown as plain text. An empty "(See:)" left
  behind by a removed translator link was dropped.
  The note is about the whole book. The screen says so. It is not presented
  as a comment written for the single verse.
  Books that this release of the Translation Notes does not include, such
  as Psalms and Isaiah, have no file. The screen says there is no note.

  with the same license and with attribution to unfoldingWord.

Today
  Charles H. Spurgeon, Faith's Checkbook (1888). Public domain.
  Source text: https://archive.spurgeon.org/fcb/fcb-bod.htm

  public/scripture/study/today/*.json keeps a reading only on the verse
  cited in that reading's heading. The date and the repeated quotation of
  the verse were left out. The title and the comment were not rewritten.
  A citation printed as "Psalm l46:8" was read as Psalm 146:8, and a
  citation with no space before the chapter, such as "Exodus8:23", was
  read as Exodus 8:23. No other verse was attached.
  A verse with no reading in this book has no today note. The screen says so.

Nothing in these files was written by a model to fill a gap.
