Matthew Henry's Commentary on the Whole Bible
Matthew Henry
Public domain. Copy freely.

This is the fallback for In context, used only when the Concise commentary
has no note for that verse. The concise notes stay in
public/scripture/meaning/. See public/scripture/meaning-license.txt.

Source text, Christian Classics Ethereal Library, labeled
"Rights: Public domain. May be copied and distributed freely."
https://ccel.org/ccel/h/henry/mhc1/cache/mhc1.txt
https://ccel.org/ccel/h/henry/mhc3/cache/mhc3.txt
https://ccel.org/ccel/h/henry/mhc5/cache/mhc5.txt

public/scripture/context/*.json keeps only the books that have a verse
the Concise commentary leaves empty: Deuteronomy, Proverbs, Matthew, and
John. The phone loads one of those books when the concise note is missing.
The whole commentary is not bundled.

A stored note is Henry's explanation of the passage he printed, not the
Bible verses printed above it. When a paragraph names a verse (v. 16,
ver. 19-20), that paragraph is the note for those verses. A paragraph
that names none keeps the verse named by the paragraph before it. When
no paragraph names a verse, the explanation of the whole printed passage
is the note. The tightest of those ranges is the one shown.
CCEL link markers and print rules were removed. The wording was not
rewritten, and it was not translated.
See scripts/build-context.mjs.

Nothing in these files was written by a model to fill a gap.
